# Breach notification

Separate legal authorities and customers/partners

# Timeline

Depending on each geography and laws.
As an example, GDPR set a 72h timeline once breach is assessed.

# Content

* When it was discovered
* Categories and approximate number of individuals concerned
* Categories and approximate number of personal data records concerned
* The name and contact details of the data protection officer
* Role of impacted company (data controller, data processor)
* Which data type are at risk (address, phone, credit cards, passwords, patient data...), age and provenance
* A description of the likely consequences of the personal data breach
* Mitigation or remediation efforts
* If credentials are involved, how they were stored (hash, salt...)
* Timeline of compromised and actions, DPA and Law Enforcement involvement
* What users can also do to protect themselves
* Point to security and privacy resources on your website (Security section, DPIA...)
* FAQ: If multiple environments impacted (corp, customer...), Impact on SSO/SAML...

# Format

Audience: public, customers, employees, investors
By: Communication, Legal, CISO, Multiple...
Frequency
Template
Channel: Email, Phone/Text Message, Web Intranet/Extranet/Public, SocialMedia, InPerson, Online meetings...

* General communication to use in press release, email with short timeline, FAQ
* Internal communication to employees
* Regular internal, external calls depending on severity

# Regular third-party reporting

* Contractual obligations, ideally standardized and terms well defined (ex: security event vs security incident vs data breach, confirmed, material...)
* EU GDPR Data Protection Authority (DPA): [Art. 33 GDPR Notification of a personal data breach to the supervisory authority](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-33-gdpr/) - 72h, [Art. 34 GDPR Communication of a personal data breach to the data subject](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-34-gdpr/)
* US CCPA, only required if a California resident’s data is actually compromised.
* US DoD DC3: [DIBNet Portal](https://dibnet.dod.mil/portal/intranet/) - DFARS 252.204-7012, DFARS 252.239-7010
* US IC3: [Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3)](https://www.ic3.gov/Home/ComplaintChoice/default.aspx)
* [Report a cyber incident, Canada](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/incident-management)
