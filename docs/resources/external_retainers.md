
Whatever the capacities of your internal team, there are cases where you will likely need help and involve external retainers. Be it for overload, advanced skills, unusual geographies or legal constraint, you should be ready to complement your team with other groups.
This should be carefully reviewed and prepared especially with
* legal team
* cyber insurance

Engagement should have a well-defined scope and objectives.

## Requirements

* Communication: Internal point(s) of contact, update frequency, channel.
* Process to provide needed access (vpn, tools, badge...)
* Preserve and collect evidence
    * Asset management/Inventory
    * Ingress/Egress points
    * Change management log
* Capacity to push software or setup network components
* Backup/Restore? BCP/DRP?
* Information sharing policy
